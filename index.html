01About

I turn repetitive, error-prone work into reliable digital workers.

As a Lead RPA Developer, I own the full automation lifecycle: assessing feasibility, writing PDDs and SDDs, architecting REFramework solutions, and leading developers through build, UAT, and hypercare. I care about automations that are maintainable, auditable, and resilient in production.

My Power Automate work complements this. I build cloud and desktop flows across SharePoint, Outlook, Teams, Excel and Dataverse, and choose the right tool for each problem rather than forcing every process into one platform.

  • Reliability first. Robust exception handling, retries and clear logging.
  • Reusable by design. Shared libraries that shorten every next build.
  • Business-led. Every bot is measured against hours saved and error rates.
